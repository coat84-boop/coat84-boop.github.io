A Little About Me

My name is Coleman Mallicoat, and I currently serve as the Director of Digital Workspace and Technology Services for the Hospital Industry Data Institute. I hold an MS in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and an MBA in IT Management, both from Western Governors University. Prior to holding this position, I spent 7 years total in Academia (technical college) as an Instructor of Satellite Communications and Information Technology. I served 13.5 years in the U.S. Army before being medically retired in January 2021. I accomplished many things during my service, and after, though I do not wish to display them on this forum. Some I'm not allowed to speak of, others I simply wish not to. I've accomplished much in my academic life, my professional life, and my amazing personal life. That being said, this course and this degree program are hopefully a pathway into permanent residence in academia, preferably at Mizzou. I wish to seek and complete my PhD in ISLT, while being an assistant professor on tenure track.

All too often we fall into this trap of; "The circumstances I've experienced are unusual, therefore the emotions and struggles they've produced in me are fundamentally inaccessible to anyone who hasn't experiencd those cicrumstances". That isn't truth, that is our mind telling us we are terminally unique. We aren't, the human experience is collective, even if the circumstances change.
