MY JOURNEY

Professional Background

Leadership, Technology & Education

Coleman Mallicoat

My name is Coleman Mallicoat, and I currently serve as the Director of Digital Workspace and Technology Services for the Hospital Industry Data Institute.

I hold a Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and an MBA in Information Technology Management, both from Western Governors University.

Before my current position, I spent seven years in technical college academia as an instructor of Satellite Communications and Information Technology.

I also served 13.5 years in the United States Army before being medically retired in January 2021. My military career shaped my approach to leadership, responsibility, and professional growth.
