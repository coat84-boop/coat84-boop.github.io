MY JOURNEY

Professional Background

Leadership, Technology & Education

Coleman Mallicoat

My name is Coleman Mallicoat, and I currently serve as the Director of Digital Workspace and Technology Services for the Hospital Industry Data Institute. In this role, I focus on improving technology operations, supporting organizational initiatives, and developing solutions that help people work more effectively.

I hold a Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and an MBA in Information Technology Management, both from Western Governors University. My educational background complements my professional experience in technology management, cybersecurity, and leadership.

Before my current position, I spent seven years in technical college academia as an instructor of Satellite Communications and Information Technology. Teaching strengthened my passion for education and helping others develop technical knowledge and practical skills.

I also served 13.5 years in the United States Army before being medically retired in January 2021. My military career shaped my approach to leadership, responsibility, and professional growth. These experiences continue to influence my belief that effective leadership begins with understanding and supporting people.
